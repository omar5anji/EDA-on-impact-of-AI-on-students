# Impact of AI on Students — Exploratory Data Analysis

An analysis of 50,000 student records exploring whether and how 
AI usage affects students academic performance.

**Dataset:** Impact of AI on Students (Kaggle)  
**Tools:** Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn

## Questions Explored
1. Does the volume of weekly AI usage correlate with GPA change?
2. Does the type of AI use case affect GPA, skill retention, 
   and perceived dependency?
3. Do debugging specific users show skill erosion with 
   increased AI usage?

## Key Finding
AI usage volume alone does not hurt grades but heavier 
use cases and higher usage hours show a very subtle negative 
trend in skill retention, suggesting the cost of AI 
misuse is almost invisible in the short term.
